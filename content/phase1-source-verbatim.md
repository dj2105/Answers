## Timeline / event content

### 22 Aug T-01

**Silence Treated as Consent to Contract**

After a customer stated they had not consented to a contract, I was instructed consent was verifiable if the customer’s record showed a contract email had been sent. Ofcom requires verifiable express consent.

GC: C1.5, C1.6

### 25 Sept T-06

**TL Closed Complaint Despite Customer Instruction**

Complaint closed suddenly despite the customer stating it should remain open until the issue was resolved. Ofcom requires complaints remain open until the customer considers it resolved.

GC: C4

### 6 Oct T-22

**Translator Refused and ADR Blocked**

A vulnerable customer stated a debt with a collection agency resulted from a language barrier. No evidence could be located. I was instructed to inform the customer the debt stood and close the complaint without mentioning Alternative Dispute Resolution. Ofcom requires access to ADR where complaints remain unresolved.

GC: C4

### 31 Oct T-29

**Vulnerable Customer Escalation**

85-year-old widow reported a store visit had resulted in a more expensive package. After a repeated humiliating in-store experience, she refused to return. Escalation resulted in the Team Leader overlooking vulnerability, offering no resolution, and closing the complaint without permission - preventing access to ADR. Ofcom requires adjusted support to safeguard vulnerable customers.

GC: C5
